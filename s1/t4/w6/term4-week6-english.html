Active & Passive Voice

Active vs Passive Voice
Active: the subject performs the action — She writes a letter.
Passive: the subject receives the action — A letter is written by her.
Passive formula: Object + auxiliary "to be" + past participle (+ by + agent)
TenseActivePassive
Present SimpleShe writes a letter.A letter is written.
Present ContinuousShe is writing a letter.A letter is being written.
Past SimpleShe wrote a letter.A letter was written.
Past ContinuousShe was writing a letter.A letter was being written.
Present PerfectShe has written a letter.A letter has been written.
Past PerfectShe had written a letter.A letter had been written.
Future SimpleShe will write a letter.A letter will be written.
ModalShe should write a letter.A letter should be written.

Section B — Forming the Passive
The verb "to be" changes with the tense; the main verb becomes a past participle.
Present Simple: is/are + pp  ·  Present Continuous: is/are + being + pp  ·  Past Simple: was/were + pp
