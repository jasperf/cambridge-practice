Section A

Particle Model & States of Matter

The particle model: all matter is made of tiny particles. In solids, particles are close together in a regular pattern and vibrate in fixed positions. In liquids, particles are close but can slide past each other. In gases, particles are far apart and move fast in all directions.
Try it — switch the state and watch the particles

Changes of State

Changes of state are physical changes — no new substance is made. The particles are the same, only the arrangement and energy change. Melting (solid→liquid), Boiling/Evaporation (liquid→gas), Condensation (gas→liquid), Freezing (liquid→solid), Sublimation (solid→gas directly).

Chemical vs Physical Changes

Physical change: no new substance is made — you can reverse it (melting ice, dissolving sugar). Chemical change (reaction): a new substance IS made — usually hard to reverse (burning, rusting). Signs of a chemical reaction: colour change, gas produced, temperature change, precipitate forms.
