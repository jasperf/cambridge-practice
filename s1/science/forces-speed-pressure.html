Speed, Distance & Time

Speed tells you how far something travels each second. speed = distance ÷ time  Rearrange it: distance = speed × time and time = distance ÷ speed. Speed is usually measured in metres per second (m/s).

Forces & Motion

A force is a push or a pull, measured in newtons (N). When the forces on an object are balanced (equal and opposite) it stays still or keeps moving at a steady speed. When forces are unbalanced, the object speeds up, slows down or changes direction in the direction of the resultant force.

Section C

Pressure

Pressure is how much force is pressing on each unit of area. pressure = force ÷ area  The same force on a smaller area gives a higher pressure (a sharp knife), and on a larger area gives a lower pressure (snowshoes, camel feet). Pressure is measured in N/m² (also called pascals, Pa).
Q13

1 mark
