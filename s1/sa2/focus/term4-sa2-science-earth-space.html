Ecosystems & Invasive Species

Ecosystem = all the living things in an area plus their non-living environment, interacting together. An invasive species is introduced to a new place and spreads, harming the native wildlife.

Earth's Layers & Climate

Earth's layers (outside → inside): crust → mantle → core. The hot, iron-rich core generates Earth's magnetic field. Fossils show that the climate of a place can change over millions of years.

Fossils are not reliable evidence of anything
