How to use this with the practice sheets: pick the topic your son lost marks on, watch one short video (5–10 min), then open the matching Topic Focus drill and try it. Use the Hint button before the Show Answer button. Re-do the sheet a day later — the aim is to score 100% from memory, not just once. Free YouTube and BBC Bitesize links need no account; documentaries marked SUB need a Netflix/Disney+ subscription. Video links occasionally change — if one has moved, search the title on YouTube.
