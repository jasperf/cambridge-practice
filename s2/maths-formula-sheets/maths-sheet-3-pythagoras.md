# Sheet 3 · Pythagoras’ Theorem

![Pythagoras’ Theorem formula sheet](svg/maths-sheet-3-pythagoras.svg)

In a right-angled triangle a² + b² = c², where c is the hypotenuse: the longest side, opposite the right angle (3² + 4² = 5², 9 + 16 = 25). To find the hypotenuse, add the squares: √(20² + 21²) = √841 = 29 cm. To find a shorter side, subtract: √(26² − 10²) = √576 = 24 cm. Look for the hidden right-angled triangle in a rectangle’s diagonal, an isosceles triangle’s height (use half the base) and a ladder against a wall. Triples to know: 3-4-5, 6-8-10, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25.
