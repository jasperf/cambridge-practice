SHEET 11 – Decimals, Rounding & Bounds
Decimals, rounding and bounds (Unit 3). Multiply decimals by counting decimal places: 0.4 × 0.03 → 4 × 3 = 12 with 3 d.p. = 0.012. Divide by making the divisor whole: 2.4 ÷ 0.08 = 240 ÷ 8 = 30; dividing by 10⁻² is the same as × 100. Significant figures start at the first non-zero digit: 0.045 82 = 0.046 (2 s.f.), 38 649 = 38 600 (3 s.f.). Bounds are half a unit either side: 6.3 cm to 1 d.p. gives 6.25 ≤ length < 6.35; 2700 to the nearest 100 gives 2650 ≤ n < 2750 (largest whole number 2749). Maximum of a + b or a × b uses both upper bounds; maximum of a − b or a ÷ b uses the upper bound of a and the lower bound of b. Percentage multipliers: +15% → × 1.15, −20% → × 0.80, +4% for 3 years → × 1.04³; reverse: after +25% it is 80, so the original is 80 ÷ 1.25 = 64.
