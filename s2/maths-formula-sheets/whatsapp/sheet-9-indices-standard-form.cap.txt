SHEET 9 – Indices, Standard Form & Rational Numbers
Indices, standard form and rational numbers (Unit 1). Index laws, same base only: multiply → add the powers (a³ × a⁴ = a⁷), divide → subtract (a⁹ ÷ a⁵ = a⁴), power of a power → multiply ((a²)⁵ = a¹⁰), a⁰ = 1, and a negative power means one over (3⁻² = 1/9, not −9). Standard form is A × 10ⁿ with 1 ≤ A < 10: 47 000 = 4.7 × 10⁴ and 0.0036 = 3.6 × 10⁻³. To calculate, deal with the numbers and the powers separately: (2 × 10³) × (6 × 10⁵) = 12 × 10⁸ = 1.2 × 10⁹; (9 × 10⁴) ÷ (3 × 10⁻²) = 3 × 10⁶. Rational numbers can be written as a fraction (0.75, 0.333…, √49); irrational numbers never end or repeat (√2, √10, π). Recurring decimal to fraction: x = 0.1818…, 100x − x = 18, so x = 18/99 = 2/11.
