# Sheet 5 · Compound Shapes

![Compound Shapes formula sheet](svg/maths-sheet-5-compound-shapes.svg)

Split a compound shape into simple shapes, then add or subtract. L-shape: 10 × 3 + 4 × 5 = 50 cm². Rectangle with a semicircle: 10 × 6 + ½ × π × 5² = 99.3 cm². Rectangle with a hole: 12 × 8 − π × 2² = 83.4 cm². Ring: π × 5² − π × 3² = 16π = 50.3 cm². Running-track shape: the two semicircles make one circle, 12 × 6 + π × 3² = 100.3 cm². Building blocks: rectangle l × w, triangle ½ × b × h, parallelogram b × h, trapezium ½ × (a + b) × h, circle π × r². To estimate, round to 1 s.f. and use π ≈ 3: r = 6.2 cm gives 3 × 6² = 108 cm².
