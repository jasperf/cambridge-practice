*MATHS FORMULAS TO KNOW* 📐

*ANGLES*
• Straight line = 180°
• Round a point = 360°
• Vertically opposite angles are equal
• Triangle = 180°
• Exterior angle = sum of the 2 opposite inside angles
• Parallel lines: F (corresponding) and Z (alternate) are equal. C (co-interior) adds to 180°

*POLYGONS* 🔷
• Sum of INTERIOR angles = (n − 2) × 180°
• Sum of EXTERIOR angles = 360° (always, any polygon)
• Regular: each exterior = 360° ÷ n, each interior = 180° − exterior
• Shape: interior sum / each interior / each exterior
• Triangle 180° / 60° / 120°
• Square 360° / 90° / 90°
• Pentagon 540° / 108° / 72°
• Hexagon 720° / 120° / 60°
• Octagon 1080° / 135° / 45°
• Decagon 1440° / 144° / 36°
• Dodecagon 1800° / 150° / 30°
• Only triangles, squares and hexagons tessellate alone (60°, 90°, 120° divide 360° exactly)

*PYTHAGORAS*
• a² + b² = c² (c = hypotenuse, the longest side)
• Hypotenuse: ADD the squares. Short side: SUBTRACT
• Triples: 3-4-5, 6-8-10, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25

*ISOSCELES TRIANGLE* 🔺
• 2 equal sides, 2 equal base angles (opposite the equal sides)
• α + 2β = 180°
• β = (180° − α) ÷ 2, α = 180° − 2β
• Height cuts the base in half at 90°
• h = √(a² − (b/2)²), so HALVE the base before Pythagoras
• Area = ½ × b × h (whole base, vertical height, NOT the slanted side)
• Example: a = 13, b = 10 → h = 12, A = 60 cm²

*SQUARE vs TRAPEZIUM* ⬜
• Square: 4 equal sides, 4 right angles, 2 pairs of parallel sides, A = s²
• Trapezium: EXACTLY 1 pair of parallel sides (a and b), legs may differ
• A = ½ × (a + b) × h
• h = perpendicular height, never the slanted leg
• Order: add the parallel sides, multiply by h, halve
• Example: a = 6, b = 10, h = 4 → ½ × 16 × 4 = 32 cm²

*CIRCLES* ⭕
• d = 2r, r = d ÷ 2 (C needs d, A needs r: double or halve first)
• C = π × d (or 2 × π × r), d = C ÷ π, π = C ÷ d
• *A = π × r²*, r² = A ÷ π, r = √(A ÷ π)
• Formula triangle: cover what you want, read off the rest
• Area gives r², so take the square root LAST
• Keep the full π until the end, round at the end
• "In terms of π": 7² × π = 49π
• Example: A = 80 → r² = 25.46… → r = 5.0 cm

*AREA* 📏
• Square s × s
• Rectangle l × w
• Triangle ½ × b × h
• Parallelogram b × h
• Trapezium ½ × (a + b) × h
• Circle π × r²
• Semicircle ½ × π × r²
• Quarter circle ¼ × π × r²
• Ring = πR² − πr²
• Compound shape: split, then add or subtract (hole = subtract)

*PERIMETER* 🔲
• Perimeter = the total distance round the OUTSIDE
• Square 4 × s
• Rectangle 2 × (l + w)
• Triangle: add all 3 sides
• Trapezium: add all 4 sides (use the slanted legs, not the height)
• Circle = circumference = π × d
• Semicircle = ½ × π × d + d (curve PLUS the straight edge)
• Quarter circle = ¼ × 2πr + 2r (curve PLUS two radii)
• Compound shape: only the outside edges, not the lines inside
• Examples: semicircle d = 10 → area 39.3, perimeter 25.7. Quarter circle r = 4 → area 12.6, perimeter 14.3

*EXPANDING BRACKETS*
• (x + a)² = x² + 2ax + a² (NOT x² + a²)
• (x + a)(x − a) = x² − a²
• Minus × minus = plus

*SIMULTANEOUS EQUATIONS*
• Same signs: SUBTRACT. Different signs: ADD
• Find both letters, then check in the other equation

*INDICES AND STANDARD FORM*
• Multiply: add powers. Divide: subtract powers
• (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ
• a⁰ = 1
• a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ (3⁻² = 1/9)
• Standard form: A × 10ⁿ with 1 ≤ A < 10
• Rational = can be a fraction. Irrational = never ends or repeats (√2, π)

*INEQUALITIES*
• < > open circle. ≤ ≥ filled circle
• Multiply or divide by a NEGATIVE: flip the sign

*ROUNDING, BOUNDS AND %*
• Bounds = half a unit either side (6.3 to 1 d.p.: 6.25 ≤ x < 6.35)
• Max of a + b or a × b: both upper bounds
• Max of a − b or a ÷ b: upper a, lower b
• +15% → × 1.15, −20% → × 0.80
• Reverse a %: ÷ the multiplier

*FORMULAE*
• Rearrange: undo the operations in reverse order
• Fractions: common denominator first
• Minus a bracket changes BOTH signs
