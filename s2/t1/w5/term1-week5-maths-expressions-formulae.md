## Section A: Substituting into Expressions

**Method:** Replace each letter with its given value, then follow the order of operations (BIDMAS/BODMAS) — powers before multiplying, multiplying before adding or subtracting.

**Example:** If x = 3, evaluate 2x + 5 → 2(3) + 5 = 6 + 5 = **11**

### Questions

**Q1** (1 mark)
If x = 5, evaluate 2x + 3.

**Q2** (1 mark)
If a = 4, evaluate a² − 5.

**Q3** (1 mark)
If p = −2, evaluate 3p + 8.

**Q4** (2 marks)
If x = 3 and y = 4, evaluate 2x² − y.

## Section B: Constructing Expressions

**Method:** Turn a word description into an algebraic expression by choosing a letter for the unknown, then translating each phrase in order.

**Example:** "A number, n, decreased by 6" → n − 6

## Section C: Expressions and Indices

**Method:** Multiply/divide the coefficients (number parts) separately from the letters, then apply the index laws to the letters — add powers when multiplying, subtract when dividing, multiply powers when raising a power to a power.

**Example:** 3x² × 4x³ = (3 × 4)x^(2+3) = **12x⁵**

## Section D: Expanding Products of Linear Expressions

**Method:** For a single bracket, multiply everything inside by the term outside: a(b + c) = ab + ac. For two brackets, multiply every term in the first bracket by every term in the second (FOIL), then collect like terms.

**Example:** (x + 2)(x + 3) = x² + 3x + 2x + 6 = **x² + 5x + 6**

## Section E: Simplifying Algebraic Fractions

**Method:** Divide the numerator and denominator by their highest common factor — a shared number, a shared letter, or both.

**Example:** (6x + 9) / 3 = **2x + 3**

## Section F: Deriving and Using Formulae

**Method:** To rearrange a formula, use inverse operations to isolate the letter you want as the subject — undo addition/subtraction first, then multiplication/division. To use a formula, substitute the given values and evaluate.

**Example:** A = ½bh, rearranged to make h the subject → **h = 2A / b**

### Questions

**Q22** (1 mark)
The formula for the area of a triangle is A = ½bh. Rearrange to make h the subject.
- A) h = A / 2b
- B) h = 2A − b
- C) h = A − 2b
- D) h = 2A / b

**Q23** (1 mark)

**Q24** (1 mark)

**Q25** (2 marks)

**Q26** (2 marks)
