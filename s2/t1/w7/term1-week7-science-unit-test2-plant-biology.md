## Part 1: Photosynthesis

**Key ideas:** **Photosynthesis** is the process by which plants use **sunlight**, **water** and **carbon dioxide** to make **oxygen** and energy in the form of a sugar, **glucose**. Plants are called **producers** because they produce their own food. Water comes from the **soil** through the roots; carbon dioxide comes from the **air**. Oxygen is released into the atmosphere and the glucose is used for biomass and energy.

**Summary word equation:**

> carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen
> *(light energy and chlorophyll are written above the arrow — they are not reactants)*

## Part 2: Chlorophyll & What Glucose Is Used For

**Key ideas:** A **chloroplast** is an organelle found only in plant cells — it is *where* photosynthesis happens. **Chlorophyll** is the green *pigment* inside the chloroplast that captures energy from sunlight. Plants use their glucose for **respiration**, **making cellulose** for cell walls, **making starch stores**, **making proteins** for growth and repair, and **making fats and oils** for storage. Extra carbohydrate is stored as **starch inside the chloroplasts**. Roots and tubers store starch/glucose; nuts and seeds store fats and oils.

## Part 3: Minerals & Plant Growth

**Key ideas:** **Minerals** are water-soluble substances that plants absorb from the **soil through their roots**. Plants need only small quantities, but if the soil runs short the plant cannot grow to its full potential. **Fertilisers** contain minerals, which is why adding them raises the **yield** — the quantity of crop harvested.

| Mineral | What it is needed for | What a shortage looks like |
|---------|----------------------|----------------------------|
| **Magnesium (Mg)** | Making chlorophyll | Leaves look **yellow** (or brown) instead of green; less light absorbed, so less photosynthesis |
| **Nitrate** (contains nitrogen) | Converting carbohydrates into **proteins**, which make new cells; also needed for chlorophyll | Plant **grows slowly and stays small**; leaves are **pale** and may die |
