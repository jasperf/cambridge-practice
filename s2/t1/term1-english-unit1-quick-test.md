**15 Open-Ended Questions for Rapid Review**

### Simple Sentences & Structure (Questions 1–3)

**Q1** | Define the two essential parts every sentence must contain.

**Q2** | Explain what makes a subject "compound."

**Q3** | Classify this sentence: "Birds sing and fly in the sky." Use SS, CS, SC, or CC.

---

### Compound Sentences — FANBOYS (Questions 4–6)

**Q4** | What does the acronym FANBOYS stand for, and what grammatical category do these words belong to?

**Q5** | Write a compound sentence using "for" to show a reason.

**Q6** | Which FANBOYS conjunction would you use to contrast these two ideas: "The technology is powerful ___ it is expensive."?

---

### Conjunctive Adverbs & Semicolons (Questions 7–9)

**Q7** | What punctuation marks are required before and after a conjunctive adverb like "however" when joining independent clauses?

**Q8** | What does MOTH stand for? Name all four words.

**Q9** | Write a compound sentence using the conjunctive adverb "therefore" with correct punctuation.

---

### Complex & Compound-Complex Sentences (Questions 10–12)

**Q10** | What is the difference between an independent clause and a dependent clause?

**Q11** | Name three common dependent markers (subordinating conjunctions).

**Q12** | Write a complex sentence using "although" with the dependent clause first. Include correct comma placement.

### P.E.E.E Framework (Questions 13–15)

**Q13** | What does each letter in P.E.E.E stand for?

**Q14** | In the P.E.E.E framework, why should you avoid simply repeating the quotation in the Explanation step?
