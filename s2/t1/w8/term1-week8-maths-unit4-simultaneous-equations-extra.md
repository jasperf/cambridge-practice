## Questions

**Q1** (3 marks)\
Solve 3x + 2y = 16 and x + 4y = 22.\
*Hint: multiply one equation so that the x terms or the y terms match.*

*x* = ______ , *y* = ______

**Q2** (3 marks)\
Solve 2x + 3y = 13 and 5x − 2y = 4.\
*Hint: this time you have to multiply both equations.*

*x* = ______ , *y* = ______

**Q3** (3 marks)\
Solve 2x + y − 7 = 0 and 3x = 4y + 5.\
*Hint: first rewrite each equation in the form ax + by = c.*

*x* = ______ , *y* = ______

**Q4** (3 marks)\
A farmer has some chickens and some goats. Together they have 20 heads and 56 legs. Write two equations, then find how many chickens and how many goats there are.

Chickens = ______ , goats = ______

**Q5** (3 marks)\
The straight line y = mx + c passes through the points (1, 5) and (3, 11).\
a) Substitute each point to write two equations in *m* and *c*.\
b) Solve them to find the equation of the line.

y = ______
