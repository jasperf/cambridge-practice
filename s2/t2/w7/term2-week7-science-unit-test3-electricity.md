## Section C: Series & Parallel Circuits

**Key ideas:** Key words for this section: **series**, **parallel**, **branch**. In *series* the current is the same everywhere and the voltage is shared. In *parallel* the current splits between branches and each branch gets the full voltage. Use the numbered circuit diagram for Q13.

**Circuit diagram (for Q13):** a loop containing ① a cell (one long and one short parallel line) on the left and ② a circle with the letter A in the top wire. ## Section D: Ohm's Law: V = I × R
