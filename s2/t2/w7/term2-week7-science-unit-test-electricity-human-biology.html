Section A · Human Biology

DNA, Genes & Chromosomes

We look a little like both of our parents because our characteristics are coded for by genetic information that we inherit from them. That information is stored inside the nucleus of our cells. Section C · Human Biology

Section F · Electricity

Ohm’s Law: V = I × R
