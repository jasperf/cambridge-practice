Section F · Electricity

Ohm’s Law: V = I × R

V = I × R, so I = V ÷ R and R = V ÷ I (cover the one you want in the formula triangle). V is in volts (V), I in amperes (A) and R in ohms (Ω). 1000 mA = 1 A. Answer the questions, then try the Ohm’s Law Circuit at the end of the section.
