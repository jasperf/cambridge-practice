*Thermal Energy Extra Questions* 🔥
6 more questions, about 10 minutes. Write your answers in your notebook. Q2 and Q5 are multiple choice.

*1.* Give three reasons why conserving heat is important in homes and buildings.

*2.* Which pair of units can be used to measure heat and temperature?
A) Heat in °C, temperature in joules
B) Heat in kelvin, temperature in calories
C) Heat in joules or calories, temperature in °C, °F or kelvin
D) Heat and temperature are both measured in joules

*3.* A wool jumper keeps you warm. Explain why, using the words trapped air, insulator and heat loss.

*4.* Name two materials used to insulate a home. Say where in the house each one is fitted.

*5.* How does better home insulation help the environment?
A) It makes the house produce more heat
B) It stops the Sun's radiation reaching the roof
C) It lets the windows stay open all day
D) The heating works less, so less energy is used and fewer greenhouse gases are released

*6.* A family complains that their house is cold and their heating bills are high. Suggest two changes to the house and explain how each one helps.
