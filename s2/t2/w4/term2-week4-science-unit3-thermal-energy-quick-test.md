# Secondary 2 · Term 2 Week 4 · Science — Thermal Energy Quick Test

**10 Questions on Heat, Temperature and How Heat Travels**

A 15-minute recall test. Write your answers in your notebook. Questions 3, 6 and 9 are multiple choice; the rest need a written answer.

---

### Heat and Temperature (Questions 1–3)

**Q1** | Explain the difference between **heat** and **temperature**. Give the unit for each.

**Q2** | A 250 mL cup and a 500 mL cup of water are both at 90°C. Which one holds more heat, and why? Mention particles in your answer.

**Q3** | Heat always flows in which direction?
- A) From the cooler object to the hotter object
- B) From the hotter object to the cooler object
- C) Both ways at the same speed, until the objects touch
- D) From the larger object to the smaller object

### Three Ways Heat Travels (Questions 4–6)

**Q4** | Name the three methods of heat transfer. For each, give one everyday example.

**Q5** | Explain how convection makes a loop of moving water in a pan being heated. Use the words *less dense*, *rise* and *sink*.

**Q6** | Why can the Sun's heat reach the Earth through empty space?
- A) Conduction passes heat along the particles between the Sun and the Earth
- B) Convection currents carry hot gas across space
- C) Space is full of air, which is a good conductor
- D) Radiation travels as infrared waves and needs no particles

### Conserving Heat (Questions 7–9)

**Q7** | Explain why a metal spoon in hot soup gets warm at the handle, but a wooden spoon stays cooler. Name the method of heat transfer.

**Q8** | Explain how double-glazed windows reduce heat loss from a house.

**Q9** | Which of these is the best way to keep a cup of tea hot for longer?
- A) Use a thin metal cup with no lid
- B) Put the cup on a metal tray
- C) Use a lidded cup with a layer of trapped air in its walls
- D) Stand the cup in front of a fan

### Putting It Together (Question 10)

**Q10** | A student says: "When I hold an ice cube, the cold flows from the ice into my hand." Correct this statement using the words *heat*, *temperature* and *particles*.
