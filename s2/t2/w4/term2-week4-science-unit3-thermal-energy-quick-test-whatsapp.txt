*Thermal Energy Quick Test* 🔥
10 questions, about 15 minutes. Write your answers in your notebook. Q3, Q6 and Q9 are multiple choice.

*HEAT AND TEMPERATURE*

*1.* Explain the difference between heat and temperature. Give the unit for each.

*2.* A 250 mL cup and a 500 mL cup of water are both at 90°C. Which one holds more heat, and why? Mention particles in your answer.

*3.* Heat always flows in which direction?
A) From the cooler object to the hotter object
B) From the hotter object to the cooler object
C) Both ways at the same speed, until the objects touch
D) From the larger object to the smaller object

*HOW HEAT TRAVELS*

*4.* Name the three methods of heat transfer. For each, give one everyday example.

*5.* Explain how convection makes a loop of moving water in a pan being heated. Use the words less dense, rise and sink.

*6.* Why can the Sun's heat reach the Earth through empty space?
A) Conduction passes heat along the particles between the Sun and the Earth
B) Convection currents carry hot gas across space
C) Space is full of air, which is a good conductor
D) Radiation travels as infrared waves and needs no particles

*CONSERVING HEAT*

*7.* Explain why a metal spoon in hot soup gets warm at the handle, but a wooden spoon stays cooler. Name the method of heat transfer.

*8.* Explain how double-glazed windows reduce heat loss from a house.

*9.* Which of these is the best way to keep a cup of tea hot for longer?
A) Use a thin metal cup with no lid
B) Put the cup on a metal tray
C) Use a lidded cup with a layer of trapped air in its walls
D) Stand the cup in front of a fan

*PUTTING IT TOGETHER*

*10.* A student says: "When I hold an ice cube, the cold flows from the ice into my hand." Correct this statement using the words heat, temperature and particles.
