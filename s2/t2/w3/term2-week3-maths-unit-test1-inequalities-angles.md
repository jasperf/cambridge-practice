## Section A: Linear Inequalities

**Method:** Solve an inequality like an equation: do the same to both sides. The one new rule: flip the inequality sign when you multiply or divide both sides by a negative number.

**Q21** (1 mark)\

- A) x = 106°, because co-interior angles add up to 180°
- B) x = 74°, because alternate angles are equal
- C) x = 74°, because corresponding angles are equal
- D) x = 106°, because alternate angles add up to 180°
