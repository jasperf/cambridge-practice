## Section A: Angle Rules

**Rules to use:** Vertically opposite angles are equal. Angles around a point add to 360°. Alternate angles between parallel lines are equal. The base angles of an isosceles triangle are equal. An exterior angle of a triangle equals the sum of the two opposite interior angles.

### Questions

**Q1** (1 mark)\
Two straight lines cross. A pair of vertically opposite angles are (5x − 15)° and 85°. Find *x*.

**Diagram:** Two straight lines crossing; the vertically opposite angles on the left and right are 5x minus 15 and 85 degrees.

Answer: x = ______

**Q2** (2 marks)\
Four angles meet at a point: a right angle, 2x°, 3x° and 4x°. Find *x*.

**Diagram:** Four rays from a point making angles of 90 degrees (a right angle), 2x, 3x and 4x.

- A) 40
- B) 20
- C) 30
- D) 45

**Q3** (2 marks)\
Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. A pair of alternate angles are (3x + 15)° and (5x − 25)°. Find *x*.

**Diagram:** Two parallel lines cut by a transversal; alternate angles 3x plus 15 (below the top line, left of the transversal) and 5x minus 25 (above the bottom line, right of the transversal).

Answer: x = ______

**Q4** (2 marks)\
An isosceles triangle has a 48° angle between its two equal sides. Find one base angle, *b*.

**Diagram:** An isosceles triangle with two equal sides marked; the angle at the top is 48 degrees and one base angle is marked b.

- A) 66°
- B) 132°
- C) 42°
- D) 48°

**Q5** (2 marks)\
The exterior angle of a triangle is (4x)°. The two opposite interior angles are 65° and (x + 25)°. Find *x*.

**Diagram:** A triangle with its base extended to the right; the exterior angle is 4x, and the two opposite interior angles are 65 degrees at the bottom left and x plus 25 at the top.

- A) 22.5
- B) 18
- C) 45
- D) 30

## Section B: Polygon Angles

**Method:** Polygon angle sum: (n − 2) × 180°. The exterior angles of any polygon add up to 360°, and at each corner interior + exterior = 180°.

### Questions

**Q6** (2 marks)\
Each interior angle of a regular polygon is 140°. How many sides does it have?

**Diagram:** Part of a regular polygon: four corners and three equal sides; one interior angle is marked 140 degrees. The number of sides is unknown.

Answer: ______ sides

**Q7** (2 marks)\
Four angles of a hexagon are 120° each. The other two angles are x° and (x + 40)°. Find *x*.

**Diagram:** A hexagon; four of its angles are 120 degrees, and the other two are x and x plus 40.

Answer: x = ______

## Section C: Circles & Compound Areas

**Method:** Area of a circle A = πr². Halve a diameter before you square it. For a compound shape, add the pieces joined on and subtract the holes. Use the π button and round only at the end.

### Questions

**Q8** (2 marks)\
Find the area of a semicircle with radius 7 cm. Give your answer to 1 decimal place.

**Diagram:** A semicircle with radius 7 cm; the radius is drawn from the centre of the straight edge.

- A) 153.9 cm²
- B) 77.0 cm²
- C) 307.9 cm²
- D) 11.0 cm²

**Q9** (2 marks)\
A circle has an area of 200 cm². Find its *diameter* to 1 decimal place.

**Diagram:** A circle with area 200 square centimetres; its diameter d is drawn across the middle.

Answer: ______ cm

**Q10** (3 marks)\
A rectangle is 14 cm long and 10 cm wide. A semicircle is cut out of one short side; its diameter is the whole 10 cm side. Find the shaded area that is left, to 1 decimal place.

**Diagram:** A 14 cm by 10 cm rectangle with a semicircle cut out of its right-hand short side; the diameter of the semicircle is the full 10 cm side.

Answer: ______ cm²
