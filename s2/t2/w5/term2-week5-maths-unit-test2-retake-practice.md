## Section A: Polygon Angles

**Remember:** Interior angle sum of an *n*-sided polygon = (*n* − 2) × 180°. The exterior angles of any polygon add to 360°. At each vertex, interior + exterior = 180°.

### Questions

**Q1** (1 mark)\
Find the sum of the interior angles of a 9-sided polygon (a nonagon).

Answer: ______°

**Q2** (2 marks)\
Each exterior angle of a regular polygon is 24°. How many sides does it have?
- A) 13
- B) 7.5
- C) 15
- D) 17

**Q3** (2 marks)\
A pentagon has angles *x*°, 2*x*°, 100°, 110° and 120°. Find *x*.

Answer: x = ______

**Q4** (2 marks)\
Each interior angle of a regular polygon is 160°. How many sides does it have?

Answer: ______ sides

## Section B: Constructions

**Remember:** With only a ruler and compasses you can draw an equilateral triangle, bisect an angle, bisect a line and draw a perpendicular. Many other angles and shapes come from combining these.

### Questions

**Q5** (1 mark)\

- A) Construct a 60° angle (from an equilateral triangle), then bisect it

## Section C: Pythagoras’ Theorem

**Remember:** In a right-angled triangle, *a*² + *b*² = *c*², where *c* is the hypotenuse (the longest side, opposite the right angle).

### Questions

**Q7** (2 marks)\
A right-angled triangle has shorter sides of 9 cm and 12 cm. Find the hypotenuse *h*.

**Diagram:** A right-angled triangle; the vertical side is 9 cm, the horizontal side is 12 cm and the sloping side is h.

Answer: h = ______ cm

**Q8** (2 marks)\
A right-angled triangle has a hypotenuse of 17 cm and one shorter side of 8 cm. Find the other shorter side *a*.

**Diagram:** A right-angled triangle; the vertical side is 8 cm, the sloping side is 17 cm and the horizontal side is a.

Answer: a = ______ cm

**Q9** (2 marks)\
A 5 m ladder leans against a vertical wall. Its foot is 1.8 m from the wall. How high up the wall does it reach? Give your answer to 1 decimal place.

**Diagram:** A ladder of length 5 m leaning against a vertical wall; the foot of the ladder is 1.8 m from the bottom of the wall, and the height it reaches up the wall is h. The wall meets the ground at a right angle.

Answer: h = ______ m

**Q10** (1 mark)\
Which three side lengths make a right-angled triangle?
- A) 6 cm, 8 cm, 11 cm
- B) 7 cm, 24 cm, 25 cm
- C) 5 cm, 11 cm, 12 cm
- D) 9 cm, 12 cm, 16 cm

## Section E: Compound Areas

**Remember:** Split a shape into rectangles, triangles, trapeziums and parts of circles. Add the pieces, or subtract the parts that are cut out. Trapezium area = ½(*a* + *b*)*h*.

### Questions

**Q15** (2 marks)\
An L-shape is made by cutting a 4 cm by 3 cm rectangle from the corner of a 10 cm by 8 cm rectangle. Find its area.

**Diagram:** A 10 cm wide, 8 cm tall rectangle with a 4 cm wide, 3 cm tall rectangle removed from its top-right corner, leaving an L-shape.

Answer: ______ cm²

**Q16** (2 marks)\
A trapezium has parallel sides of 7 cm and 13 cm and a perpendicular height of 6 cm. Find its area.

**Diagram:** A trapezium with a 7 cm top and a 13 cm bottom; a perpendicular height of 6 cm is drawn between them.

Answer: ______ cm²

**Q17** (3 marks)\
A shape is a 12 cm by 8 cm rectangle with a semicircle on its 12 cm side. Find the total area to 1 decimal place.

**Diagram:** A rectangle 12 cm wide and 8 cm tall, with a semicircle sitting on top; the diameter of the semicircle is the whole 12 cm top side.

Answer: ______ cm²

**Q18** (3 marks)\
A ring is the shaded part between two circles with the same centre. The outer radius is 9 cm and the inner radius is 5 cm. Find the area of the ring to 1 decimal place.

**Diagram:** Two circles with the same centre; the outer radius is 9 cm, the inner radius is 5 cm, and the ring between them is shaded.

Answer: ______ cm²

## Section F: Angle Revision (Unit 5.1)

**Remember:** Vertically opposite angles are equal. Angles in a triangle add to 180°. Between parallel lines: alternate and corresponding angles are equal, and co-interior angles add to 180°.

### Questions

**Q19** (2 marks)\
Two straight lines cross at the top corner of a triangle. The angle above the crossing point is 38°. The bottom-right angle of the triangle is 75°. Find the bottom-left angle *y*.

**Diagram:** Two straight lines cross; below the crossing point they form the two sloping sides of a triangle, closed by a horizontal base. The angle above the crossing point (between the two lines) is 38°. The triangle’s bottom-right angle is 75° and its bottom-left angle is y.

Answer: y = ______°

**Q20** (1 mark)\
Two lines are cut by a transversal. Two co-interior angles between them are 106° and 78°. Are the two lines parallel?
- A) Yes — both angles are less than 180°
- B) Yes — co-interior angles do not need to be equal
- C) No — the two angles add up to 184°, not 180°
- D) No — 106° and 78° are not alternate angles
