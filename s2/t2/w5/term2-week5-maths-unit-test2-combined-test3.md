## Section A: Angle Rules

**Rules to use:** Vertically opposite angles are equal. Angles on a straight line add to 180°; angles around a point add to 360°. Alternate, corresponding and co-interior (allied) angles between parallel lines. Complementary angles add to 90°.

### Questions

**Diagram:** Two straight lines crossing; the vertically opposite angles on the left and right are 4x plus 18 and 146 degrees.

Answer: x = ______

**Q2** (1 mark)\

**Diagram:** A straight line with a ray meeting it; the two adjacent angles are 132 degrees (left) and x (right).

**Q3** (1 mark)\

**Diagram:** Two parallel lines cut by a transversal; corresponding angles 130 degrees (above the top line, left of the transversal) and 5x plus 15 (above the bottom line, left of the transversal).

Answer: x = ______

**Q5** (2 marks)\

## Section B: Triangles & Polygon Angles

**Method:** Triangle angles add to 180°; base angles of an isosceles triangle are equal; an exterior angle equals the sum of the two opposite interior angles. Quadrilateral angles add to 360°. Polygon interior sum: (n − 2) × 180°. Exterior angles of any polygon add to 360°, and interior + exterior = 180°.

### Questions

**Q9** (2 marks)\
The angles of a triangle are *x*°, 4x° and 5x°. Find *x*.

**Diagram:** A triangle with angles x, 4x and 5x.

Answer: x = ______

**Q10** (2 marks)\
The angles of a triangle are (x + 30)°, (2x − 15)° and (2x + 45)°. Find *x*.

**Diagram:** A triangle with angles x plus 30, 2x minus 15 and 2x plus 45.

Answer: x = ______

**Q11** (2 marks)\

**Diagram:** An isosceles triangle with two equal sides marked; the angle at the top is 64 degrees and one base angle is marked b.

- A) 58°
- B) 26°
- C) 116°
- D) 64°

Answer: x = ______

## Section C: Circles & Compound Areas

**Method:** Area of a circle A = πr². Halve a diameter before you square it. For a compound shape, add the pieces joined on and subtract the holes. Use the π button and round only at the end.

### Questions

**Q17** (2 marks)\
Find the area of a semicircle with radius 9 cm. Give your answer to 1 decimal place.

**Diagram:** A semicircle with radius 9 cm; the radius is drawn from the centre of the straight edge.

- A) 254.5 cm²
- B) 127.2 cm²
- C) 63.6 cm²
- D) 31.8 cm²

**Q18** (2 marks)\
A circle has an area of 600 cm². Find its *diameter* to 1 decimal place.

**Diagram:** A circle with area 600 square centimetres; its diameter d is drawn across the middle.

Answer: ______ cm

**Q19** (3 marks)\
A rectangle is 20 cm long and 10 cm wide. A semicircle is cut out of one short side; its diameter is the whole 10 cm side. Find the shaded area that is left, to 1 decimal place.

**Diagram:** A 20 cm by 10 cm rectangle with a semicircle cut out of its right-hand short side; the diameter of the semicircle is the full 10 cm side.

Answer: ______ cm²

**Q20** (2 marks)\
A square has sides of 14 cm. A circle is drawn inside it, touching all four sides (diameter 14 cm). Find the area of the shaded part *between* the square and the circle, to 1 decimal place.

**Diagram:** A 14 cm square with a circle of diameter 14 cm inside it, touching all four sides; the four corners between the square and the circle are shaded.

Answer: ______ cm²
