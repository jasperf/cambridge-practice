## Part 1: Basic — Angle Types, Lines & Triangles

**Rules to use:** Vertically opposite angles are equal. Angles on a straight line add to 180°. Angles around a point add to 360°. Angles in a triangle add to 180°. Complementary angles add to 90°.

### Questions

**Diagram:** Two crossing lines; the vertically opposite angles on the left and right are 3x plus 10 and 70 degrees.

**Diagram:** A straight line with a ray making angles x and 120 degrees.

Answer: x = ______

**Diagram:** Three rays from a point making angles of 90, 140 and x degrees.

**Q4** (1 mark)\

**Diagram:** Two parallel lines cut by a transversal; corresponding angles 70 degrees and 2x plus 10, both above the line on the right of the transversal.

Answer: x = ______

**Q5** (1 mark)\

## Part 2: Intermediate — Parallel Lines & Polygons

**Rules to use:** Corresponding and alternate angles are equal. Co-interior (allied) angles between parallel lines add up to 180°. Triangle angles add to 180°.

### Questions

**Q11** (2 marks)\
Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. Two corresponding angles are (4x − 10)° and 70°. Find *x*.

**Diagram:** Two parallel lines cut by a transversal; corresponding angles 70 degrees and 4x minus 10, both above the line on the right of the transversal.

Answer: x = ______

**Q12** (2 marks)\
The angles of a triangle are (x + 10)°, 2x° and (x + 30)°. Find *x*.

**Diagram:** A triangle with angles x plus 10 at the top, 2x at bottom left and x plus 30 at bottom right.

Answer: x = ______

**Q13** (2 marks)\
Two co-interior angles between parallel lines are in the ratio 2 : 4. Find *both* angles.

**Diagram:** Two parallel lines cut by a transversal; co-interior angles 4k and 2k on the same side, between the lines.

- A) 60° and 120°
- B) 30° and 60°
- C) 90° and 180°
- D) 40° and 80°

**Q14** (2 marks)\
A triangle has angles 60°, 70° and x°. Find *x*.

**Diagram:** A triangle with angles 60 and 70 degrees at the base and x at the top.

Answer: ______ °

**Q15** (2 marks)\
The interior angles of a hexagon add up to 720°. If it is regular, find each interior angle.

**Diagram:** A regular hexagon.

- A) 120°

- D) 110°

## Part 3: Advanced — Ratios, Exterior Angles & Polygons

**Method:** For ratio questions, write the angles as k-multiples (e.g. 1k : 2k : 3k : 4k) and use the angle sum to find k. Exterior angle of a triangle = sum of the two opposite interior angles.

### Questions

**Q16** (2 marks)\
The interior angles of a quadrilateral are in the ratio 1 : 2 : 3 : 4. Find the *largest* angle.

**Diagram:** A quadrilateral whose angles are in the ratio 1 to 2 to 3 to 4.

- A) 108°
- B) 36°
- C) 144°
- D) 180°

**Q17** (2 marks)\
Two co-interior angles are (3x + 10)° and (2x + 20)°. The lines are parallel. Find *x*.

**Diagram:** Two parallel lines cut by a transversal; co-interior angles 3x plus 10 and 2x plus 20 on the same side, between the lines.

Answer: x = ______

**Q18** (2 marks)\
Two supplementary angles are such that one angle is twice the other. Find *both* angles.

**Diagram:** A straight line with a ray making an angle x and an angle twice as big, 2x.

- A) 60° and 120°
- B) 90° and 90°
- C) 45° and 135°
- D) 30° and 150°

**Q19** (2 marks)\
The exterior angle of a triangle is 120°. Its two opposite interior angles are (2x)° and (x + 30)°. Find *x*.

**Diagram:** Triangle with an exterior angle of 120 degrees; the opposite interior angles are 2x and x plus 30.

- A) 20
- B) 30
- C) 45
- D) 60

**Q20** (2 marks)\
A regular polygon has an exterior angle of 60°. Find the *number of sides*.

**Diagram:** A regular hexagon with one side extended to show an exterior angle of 60 degrees.

- A) 5
- B) 6
- C) 8
- D) 12

## Section D: Polygon Angles

**Method:** Write the rule you are using before you calculate. Polygon angle sum: (n − 2) × 180°. The exterior angles of any polygon add up to 360°, and at each corner interior + exterior = 180°.

## Section F: Compound Areas

**Method:** Split the shape into rectangles, triangles and (semi)circles. *Add* the pieces that are joined on and *subtract* the holes.

### Questions

**Diagram:** An L-shape: a 12 cm by 5 cm rectangle with a 4 cm by 3 cm rectangle on top at the left.

Answer: ______ cm²

**Q39** (2 marks)\
A rectangle is 8 cm wide and 6 cm high. A triangle with base 8 cm and height 5 cm sits on top. Find the total area.

**Diagram:** A rectangle 8 cm wide and 6 cm high with a triangle of base 8 cm and height 5 cm on top.

Answer: ______ cm²

**Q40** (2 marks)\
