## Section A: Polygon Angles

**Method:** Polygon angle sum: (n − 2) × 180°. The exterior angles of any polygon add up to 360°, and at each corner interior + exterior = 180°.

### Questions

**Diagram:** A 12-sided polygon.

Answer: ______ °

**Diagram:** A quadrilateral with its angles labelled x, 3x, 5x and 7x.

Answer: ______ °

**Q3** (2 marks)\
Three angles of a pentagon are 100° each. The other two angles are equal. Find one of the equal angles, y.

**Diagram:** A pentagon. Three of its angles are marked 100°; the other two are marked y.

## Section C: Compound Areas

**Method:** Split the shape into rectangles, triangles and (semi)circles. Add the pieces that are joined on and subtract the holes.

### Questions

**Q18** (2 marks)\
An L-shape is made from a 12 cm × 5 cm rectangle and a 4 cm × 3 cm rectangle joined along an edge (no overlap). Find its total area.

**Diagram:** An L-shape: a 12 cm by 5 cm rectangle along the bottom, with a 4 cm by 3 cm rectangle standing on its left end.

Answer: ______ cm²

**Q19** (2 marks)\
A rectangle is 8 cm wide and 6 cm high. A triangle with base 8 cm and height 5 cm sits on top. Find the total area.

**Diagram:** A house shape: a rectangle 8 cm wide and 6 cm high with a triangle on top, base 8 cm and height 5 cm.

Answer: ______ cm²

**Q20** (2 marks)\
A 10 cm × 8 cm rectangle has a circular hole of diameter 6 cm cut out. Find the remaining area to 1 decimal place.

**Diagram:** A 10 cm by 8 cm rectangle with a circular hole of diameter 6 cm cut out of the middle.

- A) 61.2 cm²
- B) 108.3 cm²
- C) 51.7 cm²
- D) −33.1 cm²

**Q21** (2 marks)\
A circle with radius 8 cm has a 4 cm × 2 cm rectangle cut out. Find the remaining area to 1 decimal place.

**Diagram:** A circle of radius 8 cm with a small 4 cm by 2 cm rectangle cut out of it.

Answer: ______ cm²

**Q22** (2 marks)\
A rectangle is 8 cm wide and 5 cm high. A semicircle with diameter 8 cm sits on top. Find the total area to 1 decimal place.

**Diagram:** A rectangle 8 cm wide and 5 cm high with a semicircle of diameter 8 cm on top.

Answer: ______ cm²
