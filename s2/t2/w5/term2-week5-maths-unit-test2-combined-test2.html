Angle Rules

Rules to use: Vertically opposite angles are equal. Angles around a point add to 360°. Alternate angles between parallel lines are equal. The base angles of an isosceles triangle are equal. An exterior angle of a triangle equals the sum of the two opposite interior angles.

Polygon Angles

Circles & Compound Areas

Method: Area of a circle A = πr². Halve a diameter before you square it. For a compound shape, add the pieces joined on and subtract the holes. Use the π button and round only at the end.
