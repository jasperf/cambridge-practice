# Secondary 2 · Mathematics — Semester 1 Mock, Paper 1C (Non-Calculator)

**Semester 1 Assessment · Thursday 26 November 2026 · Mathematics Paper 1 (third set) · 1 hour · no calculator**\
**36 questions · 50 marks** (+ 5 written questions, 19 marks)

A third non-calculator paper for one more round of practice. It has the same layout, length and mix of topics as Paper 1 (`sem1-maths-paper1-non-calculator.md`) and Paper 1B (`sem1-maths-paper1b-non-calculator.md`), but every question is different. Paper 2 (`sem1-maths-paper2-calculator.md`) allows a calculator. It covers Unit 1 (rational and irrational numbers, indices, standard form), Unit 2 (expressions and formulae), Unit 3 (decimals, percentages and rounding), Unit 4 (simultaneous equations and inequalities), Unit 5 (angles, polygons, constructions) and Unit 7.1–7.2 (circles and compound shapes). Q1–Q36 (50 marks) match the interactive sheet; allow 1 hour. Q37–Q41 are extra written questions marked by the teacher. You need a ruler, compasses and a protractor. All diagrams are described in words and are not drawn accurately.
