## Written questions: marking guide

**Q77** (30 marks): **Judul (5 marks):** a short, attractive title that names the product or promotion (1 for a title, up to 5 for a clear, attractive and well-placed one).\
**Informasi produk (15 marks):** product name (3), a description of the product (4), the price in numbers and words (4), how and where to get it, with contact details (4).\
**Kalimat ajakan (10 marks):** two or more persuasive sentences (4 each) with correct *-lah* and *-kan* verbs (1 each, 2 in total). Example: *Cobalah nasi goreng kami! Dapatkan promonya sekarang!*

**Q78** (8 marks): 1 mark for each correct sentence that uses *ada* and a preposition of place correctly. Check that five different prepositions appear and all three patterns are used (the plan and labels are not marked separately). Example: *Di atas meja guru ada buku. Ada tempat sampah di bawah meja guru. Papan tulis ada di depan kelas.*

**Q79** (8 marks): 1 mark for each correct question (apa, di mana, siapa, berapa) and 1 mark for a correct full-sentence answer. Example for Ad A: *Apa nama produknya? Nama produknya nasi goreng ayam spesial. Di mana nasi goreng tersedia? Nasi goreng tersedia di Kantin Sehat. Siapa yang bisa dihubungi? Kita bisa menghubungi Pak Rudi. Berapa harganya? Harganya mulai dari Rp12.000.*

**Q80** (10 marks): Drawing with five labelled parts (2). Verbs: *punya*, *mempunyai* and *memiliki* each used correctly once (3). *ber-* used twice correctly (2). One correct comparative sentence (2). Spelling and sentence order (1). Example: *Gajah punya belalai panjang. Gajah mempunyai empat kaki. Gajah memiliki telinga besar. Gajah berbelalai panjang. Gajah berekor pendek. Gajah lebih besar dari kucing.*
