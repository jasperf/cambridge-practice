## Section A: Di Dalam Kelasku (Unit 1: In My Classroom)

Three patterns say where something is: **preposisi + benda 1 + ada + benda 2**, **Ada + benda 1 + preposisi + benda 2** and **benda 1 + ada + preposisi + benda 2**. Prepositions of place include di atas, di bawah, di dalam, di depan, di belakang, di samping, di antara, di tengah and di dekat. Also revise classroom vocabulary, the question word *apakah* (yes/no questions), personal pronouns and possessive pronouns.

## Section B: Teks Iklan (Unit 2: Advertisements)

An advertisement (iklan) has a **judul** (title), **informasi produk** (product name, description, price, how to get it) and a **kalimat ajakan** (persuasive sentence). Its language is *singkat*, *menarik*, *informatif* and *persuasif*. Question words: **apa** (what), **di mana** (where), **siapa** (who), **berapa** (how much / how many). A command or invitation can be made with **verb + -lah** or **verb + -kan**.

**Iklan A**

> **PROMO AKHIR TAHUN: NASI GORENG AYAM SPESIAL**
>
> Nasi goreng ayam spesial dengan telur dan sayur segar. Harga mulai dari Rp12.000.
>
> Beli 2 porsi, dapat 1 es teh GRATIS!
>
> Tersedia di Kantin Sehat, Gedung B, setiap hari pukul 10.00–14.00 WIB. Bisa makan di tempat atau dibawa pulang. Siswa dapat diskon 10%.
>
> Hubungi Pak Rudi: 0812-5555-0123.
>
> Cobalah nasi goreng kami! Dapatkan promonya sekarang!

**Iklan B**

> **APARTEMEN MEWAH DEKAT KAMPUS**
>
> Cocok untuk mahasiswa dan keluarga kecil. Fasilitas: kolam renang, taman dan WiFi. Cicilan mulai dari Rp3.500.000 per bulan.
>
> Hubungi Bu Maya: 0813-7777-8899.
>
> Pilih apartemen impianmu sekarang!

**Q77** (30 marks) · *Teks Iklan*\
**Tulis iklanmu sendiri.** (Write your own advertisement.) Choose a product (for example a food, a drink, a toy or a service) and draw and write the advertisement on a page. Your ad must have: (a) a **judul** (title); (b) **informasi produk**: the product name, a short description, the price (write the price in numbers and in words) and how to get or buy it, with a contact or place; (c) **kalimat ajakan**: at least two persuasive sentences, one with *-lah* and one with *-kan*.

**Q78** (8 marks) · *Di Dalam Kelasku*\
**Kelas saya.** Draw a plan of a classroom and label 8 items. Then write **8 sentences** about where things are. Use *ada* each time, at least **five different prepositions**, and all **three sentence patterns** (preposisi + benda 1 + ada + benda 2; Ada + benda 1 + preposisi + benda 2; benda 1 + ada + preposisi + benda 2).
