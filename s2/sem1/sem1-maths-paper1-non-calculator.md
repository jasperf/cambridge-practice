# Secondary 2 · Mathematics — Semester 1 Mock, Paper 1 (Non-Calculator)

**Semester 1 Assessment · Thursday 26 November 2026 · Mathematics Paper 1 · 1 hour · no calculator**\
**36 questions · 50 marks** (+ 5 written questions, 19 marks)

The first of two mock papers for the Semester 1 maths assessment, in the Checkpoint format: Paper 1 is non-calculator and Paper 2 (`sem1-maths-paper2-calculator.md`) allows a calculator. Both papers mix every topic from Semester 1: Unit 1 (rational and irrational numbers, indices, standard form), Unit 2 (expressions and formulae), Unit 3 (decimals, percentages and rounding), Unit 4 (simultaneous equations and inequalities), Unit 5 (angles, polygons, constructions, Pythagoras) and Unit 7.1–7.2 (circles and compound shapes). Q1–Q36 (50 marks) match the interactive sheet; allow 1 hour. Q37–Q41 are extra written questions marked by the teacher. You need a ruler, compasses and a protractor. All diagrams are described in words and are not drawn accurately.
