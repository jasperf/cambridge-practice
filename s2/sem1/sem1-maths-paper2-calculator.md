# Secondary 2 · Mathematics — Semester 1 Mock, Paper 2 (Calculator)

**Semester 1 Assessment · Thursday 26 November 2026 · Mathematics Paper 2 · 1 hour · calculator allowed**\
**29 questions · 50 marks** (+ 5 written questions, 23 marks)

The second of two mock papers for the Semester 1 maths assessment, in the Checkpoint format: Paper 1 (`sem1-maths-paper1-non-calculator.md`) is non-calculator and this paper allows a calculator. Both papers mix every topic from Semester 1: Unit 1 (rational and irrational numbers, indices, standard form), Unit 2 (expressions and formulae), Unit 3 (decimals, percentages and rounding), Unit 4 (simultaneous equations and inequalities), Unit 5 (angles, polygons, constructions, Pythagoras) and Unit 7.1–7.2 (circles and compound shapes). Q1–Q29 (50 marks) match the interactive sheet; allow 1 hour. Q30–Q34 are extra written questions marked by the teacher. All diagrams are described in words and are not drawn accurately.

## Section E: Written Questions

*These questions are not on the interactive sheet and are not auto-marked. A calculator is allowed. Show all your working in your notebook.*

**Q30** (4 marks)\
The mass of the Earth is about 5.97 × 10²⁴ kg. The mass of the Moon is about 7.35 × 10²² kg. About how many times heavier is the Earth than the Moon? Show your working and give your answer to the nearest whole number.

**Q31** (4 marks)\
A town has 12 000 people. The population grows by 5% each year.

(a) Write a calculation for the population after *n* years. (1)

(b) After how many whole years will the population first be more than 15 000? Show your working. (3)

**Q32** (5 marks)\
A rectangular garden is 14 m long and 9 m wide, both correct to the nearest metre.

(a) Write the lower and upper bounds of each length. (2)

(b) Work out the smallest possible area and the largest possible area. (2)

(c) Can the owner be certain that 120 m² of grass seed is enough? Explain. (1)

**Q33** (5 marks)\
A ramp must rise 0.9 m over a horizontal distance of 4 m.

(a) Find the length of the sloping surface to 2 decimal places. (3)

(b) The builder has a 4.1 m plank. Is it long enough? Explain. (2)

**Q34** (5 marks)\
A running track has two straight sides of 85 m and two semicircular ends. The straight sides are 60 m apart.

(a) Find the distance once round the inside of the track, to the nearest metre. (3)

(b) Explain why a runner on an outer lane runs further. (2)
