Number, Percentages & Bounds

Indices & standard form sheet →
Key facts

Percentage multipliers

Increase by r%: multiply by 1 + r/100. Decrease by r%: multiply by 1 − r/100. For two changes in a row, multiply the multipliers. Do not add the percentages.

Repeated change

Value after n years = start × multipliern. Round only at the very end.

Going backwards

To find the original, divide by the multiplier. Do not add the percentage back on.

Standard form

Multiply the numbers and add the powers of 10. Then change the answer back to an ordinary number if asked.

Significant figures

Start counting at the first non-zero digit. Zeros before it, after the decimal point, are not significant.

Bounds

A value given to the nearest 50 can be up to 25 below or above. For a safe limit use the smallest possible limit and the largest possible load.

Algebra & Equations

Simultaneous equations sheet →
Key facts

Substitute carefully

Write the formula, replace each letter by its number in brackets, then use the calculator. For v² you may need a square root last.

Indices laws

Divide the numbers, then subtract the powers of each letter.

Changing the subject

Undo the operations in the reverse order, doing the same to both sides.

Inequalities

Solve like an equation, then decide whether the answer must be a whole number. Round in the direction that keeps the condition true.

Two equations

Where two lines cross, both equations are true at the same time. Eliminate a letter, then put it back to find the other.

Words to equations

Give each unknown a letter. Write one equation for each sentence of information.

Polygons & Pythagoras

Unit test 2 sheet →
Key facts

Polygon angles

Interior angle + exterior angle = 180° at each corner. The exterior angles of any polygon add to 360°.

Pythagoras

a² + b² = c², where c is the longest side, opposite the right angle.

Hypotenuse or shorter side?

Longest side: add the squares, then square root. A shorter side: subtract, then square root.

Triples

A triangle is right-angled only if the two smaller squares add to exactly the largest square.

Isosceles triangle

The line from the top, straight down to the base, makes two equal right-angled triangles and halves the base.

Circles & Compound Shapes

Unit test 2 sheet →
Key facts

Use the π button

Keep all the digits on screen until the final answer, then round as asked.

Two formulas

Circumference = π × diameter = 2πr. Area = πr². Check whether you are given the radius or the diameter.

Working backwards

To find the radius from the circumference, divide by 2π. From the area, divide by π and then square root.

Perimeter of a part-circle

Add the curved edge and every straight edge on the outside. A straight edge where two parts join is inside the shape.

Compound areas

Add the pieces, or take one piece away from a bigger shape. A ring is the large circle minus the small one.
