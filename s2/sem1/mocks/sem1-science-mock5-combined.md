## Section A: Atoms & the Periodic Table

### Key facts

- **Inside an atom:** Protons (+1) and neutrons (no charge) sit in the nucleus; electrons (−1) move in shells around it. A neutral atom has as many electrons as protons.
- **Two numbers:** atomic number = protons · mass number = protons + neutrons
- **Filling shells:** For the first 20 elements the shells hold up to 2, 8, 8, 2, filled from the inside out.
- **Reading the table:** Group = electrons in the outer shell. Period = number of shells in use.
- **Ions:** Atoms lose or gain electrons to get a full outer shell. Losing electrons gives a positive ion; gaining gives a negative ion. The number of protons never changes.

## Section B: Bonding & Chemical Structures

### Key facts

- **Ionic bonding:** Metal + non-metal. Electrons move from the metal to the non-metal; the oppositely charged ions attract. Groups 1/2/3 form 1+/2+/3+ ions; Groups 5/6/7 form 3−/2−/1−.
- **Naming and formulae:** Metal first, non-metal ending changed to *-ide*. Total positive charge = total negative charge: Al³⁺ and Cl⁻ give AlCl₃.
- **Covalent bonding:** Non-metals share pairs of electrons. Each shared pair is one single bond.
- **Metallic bonding:** Positive metal ions with electrons that are free to move through the whole structure.
- **Simple vs giant:** Simple molecules: strong bonds *inside* each molecule, but low melting and boiling points. Giant covalent structures (diamond, graphite, SiO₂): very high melting points.

## Section D: Transport, Transpiration & the Carbon Cycle

### Key facts

- **Xylem and phloem:** Xylem carries water and minerals *up* from the roots. Phloem carries dissolved sugar to wherever it is needed.
- **Root hairs:** Water enters the root hair cells from the soil by osmosis; minerals are absorbed there too.
- **Transpiration:** Water evaporates from the leaf and escapes as vapour through tiny pores opened and closed by guard cells. Faster when hot, windy, dry and bright.
- **Carbon cycle:** Photosynthesis takes carbon dioxide *out* of the air. Respiration, decomposition and burning put it *back*. Eating passes carbon along food chains; fossil fuels lock it away for millions of years.

## Section E: Human Biology: Excretion & Inheritance

### Key facts

- **Excretion:** Getting rid of waste made by the body’s own cells. The liver turns spare amino acids into urea, which the kidneys remove.
- **Urinary system:** Kidneys filter the blood and make urine, which is stored in the bladder and leaves through the urethra.
- **Chromosomes:** Genes are sections of DNA on chromosomes, which come in pairs. Gametes (egg and sperm) have half the usual number.
- **Boy or girl:** XX is female and XY is male. Every egg carries X; a sperm carries X or Y, so each baby has a 50% chance of either sex.
- **The placenta:** The mother’s and fetus’s bloods come very close in the placenta but never mix. Substances diffuse across, both ways.

## Section F: Density

### Key facts

- **The formula:** ρ = m ÷ V, rearranged to m = ρ × V and V = m ÷ ρ.
- **Volumes:** Regular block: length × width × height. Odd shape: measure how far the water rises in a measuring cylinder (1 mL = 1 cm³).
- **Units:** 1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³. Water is 1 g/cm³.
- **Float or sink:** An object floats on a liquid that is denser than it, and sinks in a liquid that is less dense.

## Section G: Sound & Waves

### Key facts

- **Measuring a wave:** Amplitude: from the middle line to the top of a crest. Wavelength: the length of one complete wave. Frequency: waves per second, in hertz (Hz).
- **Period:** T = 1 ÷ f and f = 1 ÷ T.
- **Pitch and loudness:** Larger amplitude: louder. Higher frequency: higher pitch.
- **Interference:** Where waves meet, their displacements add. Crest + crest: bigger (constructive). Crest + trough: smaller or cancelled (destructive).

## Section H: Thermal Energy & Conservation

### Key facts

- **Temperature vs thermal energy:** Temperature measures how hot something is (the average energy of its particles). Thermal energy also depends on how many particles there are.
- **Three transfers:** Conduction passes energy particle to particle. Convection is a flow of warm liquid or gas. Radiation (infrared) needs no particles at all.
- **Conservation of energy:** Energy is never created or destroyed. energy in = useful energy out + wasted energy out.
- **Efficiency:** efficiency = useful energy out ÷ total energy in × 100%.

## Section I: Electricity

### Key facts

- **Ohm’s law:** V = I × R, so I = V ÷ R and R = V ÷ I.
- **Series:** One loop: the current is the same everywhere and resistances add up.
- **Parallel:** Each branch gets the full battery voltage, and the branch currents add up to the current from the battery.
- **Meters:** An ammeter is connected in series; a voltmeter in parallel, across the component.
