## Section A: Electricity: Series Circuits, Symbols & Resistance

### Key facts

- **Current in series:** There is only one path, so the current is the *same* at every point in the loop.
- **Voltage in series:** The cell’s voltage is *shared* between the components. Their voltages add up to the cell voltage.
- **Resistance:** R = V ÷ I   resistance in ohms (Ω), voltage in volts, current in amps.
- **Variable resistor:** Its resistance can be changed by sliding a contact. Used in dimmer switches and volume controls.

## Section B: Inheritance, Cell Division & Pregnancy

### Key facts

- **Chromosome numbers:** Body cells hold chromosomes in pairs (two sets). Sex cells hold *one of each pair* (one set).
- **Two kinds of division:** *Mitosis* copies a cell exactly. *Meiosis* halves the chromosome number to make sex cells.
- **Fertilisation:** The nucleus of a sperm joins the nucleus of an egg, so the new cell has the full number of chromosomes again.
- **In the uterus:** The fetus is joined to its mother by the umbilical cord. Harmful substances in her blood, such as alcohol, can reach it.

## Section C: Sound Waves

### Key facts

- **Wavelength:** The distance from one point on a wave to the same point on the next wave.
- **Period:** The time for one complete wave, in seconds. frequency = 1 ÷ period
- **Passing it on:** Sound is carried by particles bumping into the particles next to them.
- **Interference:** When two waves meet they add together. In step they make a bigger wave; out of step they can cancel.

## Section D: Density

### Key facts

- **The formula:** density = mass ÷ volume rearranged: mass = density × volume
- **Overflow can:** Fill it to the spout, lower the object in and catch the water that spills out. That water has the same volume as the object.
- **Float or sink:** Less dense than the liquid: floats. Denser: sinks.
- **Liquids that do not mix:** They settle into layers, one on top of another, in order of density.

## Section E: Thermal Energy & Investigations

### Key facts

- **Conduction:** Particles pass vibrations on to their neighbours. Metals conduct best; plastic, wood and trapped air are insulators.
- **Convection:** Only in liquids and gases. Warm fluid rises; cooler fluid sinks to take its place.
- **Radiation:** Infrared waves, given out by every warm object. The colour and shine of a surface change how much it gives out.
- **Fair tests:** Change one variable, measure one, keep all the others the same. An *anomalous* result does not fit the pattern: leave it out of the mean.
