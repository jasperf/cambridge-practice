## Key formulas and facts

- **Atoms:** neutrons = mass number − atomic number. Shells hold 2, 8, 8
- **Ions:** metals lose electrons (+), non-metals gain electrons (−). Charges must cancel to zero
- **Photosynthesis:** carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen (needs light and chlorophyll)
- **Chromosomes:** body cell 46 (diploid), gamete 23 (haploid). Mitosis makes copies, meiosis makes gametes
- **Sex of a baby:** XX girl, XY boy. The sperm decides, 50% chance each
- **Density:** ρ = m ÷ V (g/cm³). Water = 1 g/cm³: more than 1 sinks, less than 1 floats
- **Displacement:** volume of object = water level after − water level before
- **Frequency:** f = number of waves ÷ time (Hz). Bigger amplitude = louder, higher frequency = higher pitch
- **Heat:** conduction in solids, convection in liquids and gases, radiation needs no medium
- **Ohm's law:** V = I × R, I = V ÷ R, R = V ÷ I (volts, amps, ohms)
- **Series:** same current everywhere, voltages add up, R total = R₁ + R₂. **Parallel:** same voltage on each branch, currents add up
- **Meters:** ammeter in series, voltmeter in parallel
