## Section A: Words to Expressions in *n*

**Q1** (2 marks): Expression: 5(*n* + 6). When n = 4: 5 × 10 = 50.

**Q2** (2 marks): Expression: (2*n*)³ + 7. When n = 2: 4³ + 7 = 64 + 7 = 71.

**Q3** (2 marks): Expression: (*n*/2)³ − 1. When n = 6: 3³ − 1 = 27 − 1 = 26.

**Q4** (2 marks): Fixed fee $9, plus $5 a day. 9 + 5 × 10 = 59.

**Q5** (1 mark): Expression: 9 + 5*d*. When d = 20: 9 + 100 = 109.

## Section B: Simplifying Surds

**Q6** (1 mark): √90 = √(9 × 10) = 3√10, so a = 3. Check: 3² × 10 = 90.

**Q7** (1 mark): √72 = √(36 × 2) = 6√2, so a = 6. Check: 6² × 2 = 72.

**Q8** (1 mark): √18 = √(9 × 2) = √9 × √2 = 3√2, so a = 3. Check: 3² × 2 = 18, but 9² × 2 = 162.

**Q9** (2 marks): (4√3)² = 4² × (√3)² = 16 × 3 = 48.

**Q10** (2 marks): √12 = 2√3 and √27 = 3√3. Sum: 5√3, so a = 5.

**Q11** (1 mark): √175 = √(25 × 7) = 5√7, so a = 5.

## Section C: Surds in Brackets

**Q12** (1 mark): √8 = √(4 × 2) = 2√2, so √8 + 5 = 2√2 + 5 and a = 2. You cannot add 2√2 and 5.

**Q13** (2 marks): 7 − 2√7 + 2√7 − 4 = 7 − 4 = 3.

**Q14** (1 mark): (√n + 4)(√n − 4) = n − 16. When n = 40: 40 − 16 = 24.

**Q15** (2 marks): √n × √n = n, the middle terms cancel, and 9 × 9 = 81. So the answer is n − 81, and k = 81.

## Section D: Zero, Negative & Powers of 10

**Q16** (1 mark): 4⁻² = 1/4² = 1/16 = 0.0625.

**Q17** (2 marks): y = 1 − 0.1 + 0.01 = 0.91.

**Q18** (2 marks): 350 ÷ 10⁻² = 350 × 10² = 35 000.

**Q19** (1 mark): 0.001 ÷ 0.00001 = 100. So 10⁻³ is the bigger one, by a factor of 100.

**Q20** (2 marks): 0.07 × 10⁴ = 700, so dividing by 10⁻⁴ gives 700 and k = −4.

**Q21** (1 mark): 6000 ÷ 10 000 = 0.6, so 10⁴ and k = 4.

## Section E: Indices & Fractions

**Q22** (1 mark): 15 ÷ 3 = 5 and a⁶ ÷ a² = a⁴, so k = 4.

**Q23** (2 marks): 10/15 = 2/3 and a²⁻⁹ = a⁻⁷ = 1/a⁷. So the answer is 2/(3a⁷), and k = 7.

**Q24** (2 marks): Top: 24n⁸. Bottom: (2n²)³ = 8n⁶. Combine: 3n². So a = 3, b = 2 and a + b = 5.

**Q25** (1 mark): (2n²)³ = 2³ × n⁶ = 8n⁶, so a + b = 8 + 6 = 14.

## Section F: Brackets, Signs & Equations

**Q26** (1 mark): x² + 2x + 6x + 12 = x² + 8x + 12, so b = 8.

**Q27** (2 marks): y² − 5y + 8y − 40 = y² + 3y − 40, so b = 3.

**Q28** (2 marks): 6y² − 8y + 9y − 12 = 6y² + y − 12, so b = 1.

**Q29** (1 mark): 3(4y + 2) = 12y + 6 and 6(2y + k) = 12y + 6k, so 6k = 6 and k = 1.

**Q30** (2 marks): (4x + 5y) − (4x − 2y) = 7y and 26 − 12 = 14. So 7y = 14, a = 7, c = 14 and a + c = 21.

**Q31** (2 marks): 7y = 14 gives y = 2. Then 4x + 10 = 26, so x = 4. Check in the second: 16 − 4 = 12. x + y = 6.

## Section G: Mixed Numbers & Powers

**Q32** (2 marks): (3/2)² = 9/4 = 2.25. 8/3 ÷ 4/3 = 2. Total: 4.25.

**Q33** (1 mark): 2½ = 5/2 and (5/2)² = 25/4 = 6.25.

**Q34** (2 marks): (5/2)² = 6.25. 7/4 × 2/7 = 0.5. So 6.25 − 0.5 = 5.75.

## Written questions: marking guide

**Q35** (3 marks): Top: 3 × 8 = 24 and n² × n⁵ = n⁷, so 24n⁷ [1]. Bottom: (2n³)² = 4n⁶ [1]. Combine: 24n⁷ ÷ 4n⁶ = 6n [1].

**Q36** (3 marks): √98 = 7√2 [1]; √50 = 5√2 and √32 = 4√2 [1]; 7 + 5 − 4 = 8, so 8√2 [1].

**Q37** (4 marks): (a) x² − 2x + 7x − 14 [1] = x² + 5x − 14 [1]. (b) 6y² + 15y − 4y − 10 [1] = 6y² + 11y − 10 [1].

**Q38** (3 marks): (a) 8 + 6k [1]. (b) 8 + 6k = 62 [1], 6k = 54, so k = 9 km [1].

**Q39** (3 marks): Subtract the second from the first: 3x = 12 [1], so x = 4 [1]. Then 8 + 3y = 17, y = 3, and the check 5(4) + 3(3) = 29 works [1].

**Q40** (3 marks): (a) x⁻¹ means 1/x, so 5⁻¹ = 1/5 = 0.2, not 5 [1]. (b) 1 − 0.2 + 0.04 [1] = 0.84 [1].

**Q41** (3 marks): ((n − 5)/4)² [1]. Brackets are needed because the dividing and squaring apply to the whole (n − 5), and squaring means power 2, not a square root [1]. When n = 13: (8/4)² = 2² = 4 [1].
