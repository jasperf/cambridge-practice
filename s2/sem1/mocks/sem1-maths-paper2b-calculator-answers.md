## Section A: Number, Percentages & Bounds

**Q1** (2 marks): 6.2 × 4.5 = 27.9 and 10⁴ × 10⁻³ = 10¹. So 27.9 × 10 = 279.

**Q2** (2 marks): 60 × 1.25 = 75. Then 75 × 0.9 = 67.50. Final price $67.50 (not $60 plus 15%).

**Q3** (1 mark): **D**: *V* × 0.92⁴ Keeping 92% each year means multiplying by 0.92 four times: *V* × 0.92⁴. A multiplies by 4 instead of using a power, B takes off 8% four times as a flat 32%, and C grows by 8%.

**Q4** (2 marks): 3200 × 1.05³ = 3200 × 1.157625 = $3704.40.

**Q5** (1 mark): The 2 significant figures are 6 and 3. The next digit is 5, so round up: 0.0064.

**Q6** (2 marks): 336 ÷ 0.8 = $420. (Adding 20% to 336 would give 403.20, which is wrong.)

**Q7** (3 marks): Lowest limit = 1975 kg. Heaviest crate = 72.5 kg. 1975 ÷ 72.5 = 27.2, so 27 crates (28 × 72.5 = 2030, too heavy).

## Section B: Algebra & Equations

**Q8** (2 marks): v² = 9 + 2 × 2.5 × 10 = 59. v = √59 = 7.68… = 7.7.

**Q9** (1 mark): **A**: 3*a*²*b*² 12 ÷ 4 = 3, a³⁻¹ = a² and b⁴⁻² = b². B subtracts the numbers, C adds the powers, and D forgets to subtract the 1 from the power of a.

**Q10** (2 marks): 60 = ½(7 + b) × 8 = 4(7 + b). So 7 + b = 15 and b = 8.

**Q11** (2 marks): 12h ≤ 75, so h ≤ 6.25. Whole hours only, so 6 (7 hours would cost $109).

**Q12** (2 marks): 3x − 2 = x + 6, so 2x = 8 and x = 4. Then y = 4 + 6 = 10. The lines meet at (4, 10).

**Q13** (1 mark): **B**: 3*p* + 2*b* = 13 and *p* + *b* = 5 Three pens (3*p*) and two books (2*b*) cost $13, and one of each costs $5. A swaps the totals, C writes a product, and D swaps the numbers of pens and books.

**Q14** (2 marks): From p + b = 5: p = 5 − b. Then 3(5 − b) + 2b = 13, so 15 − b = 13 and b = 2, p = 3. Cost = 2 × 3 + 5 × 2 = $16.

## Section C: Polygons & Pythagoras

**Q15** (2 marks): **B**: 15 Exterior angle = 180 − 156 = 24°. Number of sides = 360 ÷ 24 = 15. The number 24 is the exterior angle, not the number of sides.

**Q16** (2 marks): 7² + 11² = 49 + 121 = 170. √170 = 13.04… = 13.0 cm.

**Q17** (2 marks): 13.5² − 8.4² = 182.25 − 70.56 = 111.69. √111.69 = 10.57… = 10.6 cm.

**Q18** (1 mark): **C**: 7, 24, 25 7² + 24² = 49 + 576 = 625 = 25². For A, 25 + 100 = 125 ≠ 144. For B, 36 + 64 = 100 ≠ 121. For D, 81 + 144 = 225 ≠ 256.

**Q19** (3 marks): Half the base = 6 cm. h = √(10² − 6²) = √64 = 8 cm. Area = ½ × 12 × 8 = 48 cm².

## Section D: Circles & Compound Shapes

**Q20** (2 marks): r = 40 ÷ (2π) = 6.366… = 6.4 cm.

**Q21** (2 marks): r² = 120 ÷ π = 38.197… and r = 6.18… = 6.2 m.

**Q22** (2 marks): Curved edge = ½ × π × 10 = 15.708. Add the diameter: 15.708 + 10 = 25.7 cm.

**Q23** (2 marks): Rectangle = 12 × 8 = 96. Semicircle radius 4: ½ × π × 4² = 25.13. Total = 121.1 cm².

**Q24** (2 marks): Area = π(8² − 5²) = π × 39 = 122.5 cm².

**Q25** (2 marks): **A**: 13.7 cm² Square = 64. Circle radius 4: π × 4² = 50.27. Corners = 64 − 50.27 = 13.7 cm². B is the circle itself, C uses a semicircle and D doubles the answer.

**Q26** (3 marks): Circumference = π × 66 = 207.35 cm. 100 000 ÷ 207.35 = 482.3, so 482 complete turns.
