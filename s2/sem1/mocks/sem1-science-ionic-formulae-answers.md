# Answers — Ionic Formula Practice

**10 marks** (1 mark each; capitals and subscripts must be correct).

| Q | Ions | Formula | Working |
|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Na⁺ + Cl⁻ | **NaCl** | Na⁺ and Cl⁻ have equal and opposite charges, so one of each: NaCl. |
| Q2 | Mg²⁺ + O²⁻ | **MgO** | 2+ and 2− cancel with one of each. Do not write Mg₂O₂: always give the simplest ratio, MgO. |
| Q3 | Mg²⁺ + F⁻ | **MgF₂** | One Mg²⁺ needs two F⁻ to balance: MgF₂. |
| Q4 | Na⁺ + O²⁻ | **Na₂O** | Two Na⁺ (total 2+) balance one O²⁻: Na₂O. |
| Q5 | Al³⁺ + Cl⁻ | **AlCl₃** | One Al³⁺ needs three Cl⁻: AlCl₃. |
| Q6 | K⁺ + S²⁻ | **K₂S** | Two K⁺ (2+) balance one S²⁻: K₂S. |
| Q7 | Li⁺ + N³⁻ | **Li₃N** | Three Li⁺ (3+) balance one N³⁻: Li₃N. |
| Q8 | Al³⁺ + O²⁻ | **Al₂O₃** | Al³⁺ × 2 = 6+ and O²⁻ × 3 = 6−, so Al₂O₃. |
| Q9 | Ca²⁺ + N³⁻ | **Ca₃N₂** | Ca²⁺ × 3 = 6+ and N³⁻ × 2 = 6−, so Ca₃N₂. |
| Q10 | Fe³⁺ + O²⁻ | **Fe₂O₃** | Fe³⁺ × 2 = 6+ and O²⁻ × 3 = 6−, so Fe₂O₃. |
