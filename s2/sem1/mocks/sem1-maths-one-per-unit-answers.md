**Q1** (2 marks): **B**: 1.8 × 10³. 6 × 3 = 18 and 10⁴ × 10⁻² = 10², so 18 × 10². The front number must be below 10, so move one 10 across: 1.8 × 10³. Option A has the right value but is not in standard form. C and D slip on the multiplication.

**Q2** (2 marks): *a* = (13² − 5²) ÷ (2 × 8) = (169 − 25) ÷ 16 = 144 ÷ 16 = **9**.

**Q3** (2 marks): 90 ÷ 1.2 = **$75**. (Taking 20% off $90 gives $72, which is the common wrong answer.)

**Q4** (2 marks): Adding the equations: 4*x* = 20, so *x* = 5. Then 5 − *y* = 1 gives *y* = 4. *x* × *y* = **20**. Check: 3(5) + 4 = 19.

**Q5** (2 marks): **D**: *x* ≤ −4. −3*x* ≥ 12, and dividing by −3 flips the sign. Test: *x* = −5 gives 15 + 4 = 19 ≥ 16. A and B forget to flip the sign, and C flips it but gets the sign of the number wrong.

**Q6** (2 marks): Exterior angle = 180° − 156° = 24°. Sides = 360 ÷ 24 = **15**.

**Q7** (2 marks): **A**: 254.5 cm². *r* = 18 ÷ 2 = 9, so area = π × 9² = 254.5 cm². B is the circumference (π × 18), C squares the diameter (π × 18²), and D is half the correct area.

**Q8** (2 marks): **C**: 47.4 cm². Rectangle = 10 × 6 = 60. Hole = π × 2² = 12.57. Left = 60 − 12.57 = 47.4 cm². A adds the hole, B forgets to square the radius (60 − 2π), and D forgets π (60 − 4).
