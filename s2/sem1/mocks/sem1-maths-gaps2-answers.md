## Section C: Surds in Brackets

**Q12** (1 mark): √18 = √(9 × 2) = 3√2, so a = 3. You cannot subtract 4 from 3√2.

**Q13** (2 marks): 11 − 3√11 + 3√11 − 9 = 11 − 9 = 2.

**Q15** (2 marks): √n × √n = n, the middle terms cancel, and 7 × 7 = 49. So the answer is n − 49, and k = 49.

**Q16** (2 marks): **D**: Yes. It simplifies to *n* − 9, which is always an integer. The middle terms cancel, leaving n − 9. An integer minus 9 is always an integer, even when n is not a square number. Try n = 12: 12 − 9 = 3.
