## Formulas to know

**Angles**
- Straight line = 180°; round a point = 360°
- Vertically opposite angles are equal
- Triangle = 180°; exterior angle = the 2 opposite inside angles
- Parallel lines: F (corresponding) and Z (alternate) are equal; C (co-interior) adds to 180°

**Polygons**
- Sum of **interior** angles = (n − 2) × 180°
- Sum of **exterior** angles = 360° (any polygon)
- Regular: exterior = 360° ÷ n; interior = 180° − exterior
- Only triangles, squares and hexagons tessellate alone

**Pythagoras**
- a² + b² = c² (c = hypotenuse). Hypotenuse: ADD the squares. Short side: SUBTRACT
- Triples: 3-4-5, 6-8-10, 5-12-13, 8-15-17, 7-24-25

**Isosceles triangle**
- α + 2β = 180°, so β = (180° − α) ÷ 2
- h = √(a² − (b/2)²): halve the base first
- Area = ½ × b × h (vertical height, not the slanted side)

**Square vs trapezium**
- Square: 4 equal sides, 2 pairs of parallel sides, A = s²
- Trapezium: exactly 1 pair of parallel sides, A = ½(a + b) × h; h is the perpendicular height, never the slanted leg

**Circles**
- d = 2r, r = d ÷ 2
- C = π × d; d = C ÷ π
- A = π × r²; r = √(A ÷ π). C uses the diameter, A uses the radius; take the square root last

**Area**
- Rectangle l × w; triangle ½ × b × h; parallelogram b × h
- Trapezium ½(a + b) × h; circle πr²
- Semicircle ½πr²; quarter circle ¼πr²; ring πR² − πr²
- Compound shape: split, then add; a hole is subtracted

**Perimeter**
- The total distance round the OUTSIDE
- Rectangle 2(l + w); square 4s; circle π × d
- Semicircle = ½πd + d (curve plus straight edge)
- Quarter circle = ¼ × 2πr + 2r (curve plus two radii)

**Expanding brackets**
- (x + a)² = x² + 2ax + a² (not x² + a²)
- (x + a)(x − a) = x² − a²
- Minus × minus = plus

**Simultaneous equations**
- Same signs: SUBTRACT. Different signs: ADD
- Find both letters, then check in the other equation

**Indices and standard form**
- Multiply: add powers. Divide: subtract powers. (aᵐ)ⁿ = aᵐⁿ
- a⁰ = 1; a⁻ⁿ = 1/aⁿ
- Standard form: A × 10ⁿ with 1 ≤ A < 10
- Rational = can be a fraction; irrational = never ends or repeats

**Inequalities**
- < and > open circle; ≤ and ≥ filled circle
- Multiply or divide by a NEGATIVE: flip the sign

**Rounding, bounds and percentages**
- Bounds = half a unit either side (6.3 to 1 d.p.: 6.25 ≤ x < 6.35)
- Max of a + b or a × b: both upper bounds. Max of a − b or a ÷ b: upper a, lower b
- +15% → × 1.15; −20% → × 0.80; reverse a %: divide by the multiplier

**Formulae**
- Rearrange: undo the operations in reverse order
- Algebraic fractions: common denominator first
- Minus a bracket changes BOTH signs
