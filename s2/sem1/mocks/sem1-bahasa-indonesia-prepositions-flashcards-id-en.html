Prepositions of Place
Flashcards · Indonesian → English

11 flashcards: the prepositions of place (on, under, in front of, between, behind, beside, near and more) plus left and right side. Say the answer out loud, then tap the card to flip it. Mark the ones you know and they disappear, so only the tricky ones are left. Want the other way round? Try English → Indonesian, or take the prepositions test. Nothing here is scored.

Remember: every one of these starts with di (“at / in / on”), so learn the second word: atas (top), bawah (bottom), dalam (inside), depan (front), belakang (back). Di samping and di dekat are close in meaning: beside vs near.
