Q1–Q10 · Atomic bonding

Combine the ions

Method
Total positive charge must equal total negative charge.
Worked example: Ca²⁺ and Cl⁻ — two Cl⁻ are needed to balance one Ca²⁺, so the formula is CaCl₂.

Quick check: swap the charge numbers and use them as subscripts, then reduce if both are divisible (Mg²⁺ + O²⁻ swaps to Mg₂O₂, which reduces to MgO).

For the small numbers, tap the subscript buttons or just type the digit: a typed 2 turns into ₂ automatically.
