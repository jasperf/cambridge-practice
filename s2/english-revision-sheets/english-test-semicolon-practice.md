# Secondary 2 · English — Semicolon Practice Test

**15 multiple-choice questions · 15 marks · about 15 minutes**

Name: ____________________  Date: ______________  Score: ____ / 15

Circle one letter for each question. Some sentences need a semicolon, some need a comma, some need both, and some need neither. Check your answers with the key on the last page.

Remember: a semicolon joins two clauses that could each stand alone. A comma goes before a FANBOYS word. After a conjunctive adverb (however, therefore, moreover, otherwise) comes a comma.

## Part A: Choose the correct sentence

**Q1**
Which sentence is punctuated correctly?
- A) The dogs were hungry, they had not eaten all day.
- B) The dogs were hungry. they had not eaten all day.
- C) The dogs were hungry; they had not eaten all day.
- D) The dogs were hungry; and they had not eaten all day.

**Q2**
Which sentence is punctuated correctly?
- A) We stayed indoors because the rain was heavy.
- B) We stayed indoors; because the rain was heavy.
- C) We stayed indoors, because; the rain was heavy.
- D) We stayed indoors; because, the rain was heavy.

**Q3**
Which sentence is punctuated correctly?
- A) I wanted to go to the park; but it started to rain.
- B) I wanted to go to the park but, it started to rain.
- C) I wanted to go to the park; but, it started to rain.
- D) I wanted to go to the park, but it started to rain.

**Q4**
Which sentence is punctuated correctly?
- A) Mia loves football, however, she dislikes training.
- B) Mia loves football; however, she dislikes training.
- C) Mia loves football; however she dislikes training.
- D) Mia loves football however; she dislikes training.

**Q5**
Which sentence is punctuated correctly?
- A) We were tired; so, we went home early.
- B) We were tired, so we went home early.
- C) We were tired so; we went home early.
- D) We were tired, so, we went home early.

## Part B: Spot the odd one out

**Q6**
Which sentence does **NOT** need a semicolon?
- A) Although it was late, we kept walking.
- B) The sky darkened; the wind rose.
- C) She was tired; nevertheless, she finished.
- D) I like tea; my sister likes coffee.

**Q7**
Which sentence uses a semicolon **incorrectly**?
- A) The lake was calm; the sky was clear.
- B) He studied hard; therefore, he passed.
- C) When the bell rang; the students left.
- D) It was cold; however, we went swimming.

**Q8**
Which punctuation correctly fills the gap?
*Plastic harms the ocean ___ we must reduce waste.*
- A) , therefore
- B) ; therefore
- C) , therefore,
- D) ; therefore,

**Q9**
Which sentence is punctuated correctly?
- A) For the picnic we packed sandwiches, apples, juice and cake.
- B) For the picnic we packed; sandwiches, apples, juice and cake.
- C) For the picnic we packed sandwiches; apples; juice and cake.
- D) For the picnic we packed sandwiches, apples; juice, and cake.

**Q10**
Which version of this sentence is correct?
*Hurry up otherwise we will miss the film.*
- A) Hurry up, otherwise we will miss the film.
- B) Hurry up; otherwise we will miss the film.
- C) Hurry up; otherwise, we will miss the film.
- D) Hurry up otherwise; we will miss the film.

## Part C: Mistakes and mixed punctuation

**Q11**
Which sentence is a **comma splice** (two clauses wrongly joined by only a comma)?
- A) The film was long, but we enjoyed it.
- B) The film was long, we enjoyed it.
- C) The film was long; we enjoyed it.
- D) The film was long, and we enjoyed it.

**Q12**
Which sentence is punctuated correctly?
- A) The park is free, moreover it has a playground.
- B) The park is free; moreover it has a playground.
- C) The park is free moreover, it has a playground.
- D) The park is free; moreover, it has a playground.

**Q13**
Which sentence uses commas **and** a semicolon correctly?
- A) Although the road was icy, the bus kept going; however, it arrived late.
- B) Although the road was icy; the bus kept going, however, it arrived late.
- C) Although the road was icy, the bus kept going, however; it arrived late.
- D) Although the road was icy; the bus kept going; however it arrived late.

**Q14**
Which sentence uses commas **and** a semicolon correctly?
- A) It rained all morning; so we stayed in, we played games, and we ate lunch.
- B) It rained all morning, so we stayed in, we played games; and we ate lunch.
- C) It rained all morning, so we stayed in; we played games, and we ate lunch.
- D) It rained all morning so we stayed in; we played games and, we ate lunch.

**Q15 (stretch)**
Sometimes a semicolon separates list items that already contain commas. Which sentence is correct?
- A) We visited Paris, France, Rome, Italy, and Madrid, Spain.
- B) We visited Paris, France; Rome, Italy; and Madrid, Spain.
- C) We visited Paris; France, Rome; Italy, and Madrid; Spain.
- D) We visited Paris, France; Rome; Italy; and Madrid, Spain.

## Answer key

| Q | Ans | Why |
|---|-----|-----|
| 1 | C | Two related clauses joined by a semicolon alone. A comma alone is a comma splice. |
| 2 | A | "Because the rain was heavy" is a dependent clause, so no semicolon and no comma. |
| 3 | D | Comma before the FANBOYS word "but". |
| 4 | B | Semicolon before "however", comma after it. |
| 5 | B | Comma before the FANBOYS word "so". |
| 6 | A | "Although it was late" is a dependent clause, so a comma (not a semicolon) follows it. |
| 7 | C | "When the bell rang" cannot stand alone, so it cannot be joined by a semicolon. |
| 8 | D | Semicolon before "therefore", comma after it. |
| 9 | A | A simple list needs only commas (and "and"). No semicolons. |
| 10 | C | Semicolon before "otherwise", comma after it. |
| 11 | B | Two full clauses joined by only a comma. |
| 12 | D | Semicolon before "moreover", comma after it. |
| 13 | A | Comma after the opening dependent clause, then a semicolon before "however" and a comma after it. |
| 14 | C | Comma before "so", a semicolon between the two closely related sentences, and a comma before "and". |
| 15 | B | Each place and country pair is separated by a comma inside, and the pairs are separated by semicolons. |

**Scoring:** 13–15 excellent · 10–12 good · below 10 read the Semicolon Usage sheet again and retry.
