# English Sheet 1 · Persuasive & Emotive Language

![Persuasive & Emotive Language sheet](svg/english-sheet-1-persuasive-emotive-language.svg)

One-page summary for the Level 2/3 English Unit Test 1 (Wednesday 28 October 2026). It lists the twelve persuasive techniques from Ms. Atiya's posters with a short meaning and a new example each, and highlights this week's focus: rhetorical questions and emotive language. Emotive words are shown as neutral word, emotive word and the feeling created (kept in cages → trapped in cages → sympathy). To analyse a technique, write Technique – Evidence – Purpose – Effect: name it, quote it, say why the writer used it and what the reader feels. Model: the writer uses a rhetorical question, "Will you turn your back on them?", to pressure the reader into helping, which makes the reader feel guilty and want to act.
