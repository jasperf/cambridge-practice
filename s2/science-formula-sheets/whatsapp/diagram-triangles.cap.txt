DIAGRAM – Formula Triangles
Formula triangles. Cover the quantity you want and read off what is left. Density: m on top, ρ and V below, so ρ = m ÷ V, m = ρ × V, V = m ÷ ρ. Ohm's law: V on top, I and R below, so V = I × R, I = V ÷ R, R = V ÷ I. For waves, period and frequency are inverses: T = 1 ÷ f and f = 1 ÷ T. Worked examples: m = 120 g and V = 40 cm³ give ρ = 3 g/cm³; T = 0.25 s gives f = 4 Hz; V = 12 V and I = 3 A give R = 4 Ω.
