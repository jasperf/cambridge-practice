## Sheet 1 · Atomic Structure & Periodic Table (T1 Unit 1)

| Quantity | Formula / rule |
|---|---|
| Atomic number | = number of protons = number of electrons (neutral atom) |
| Mass number | = protons + neutrons |
| Neutrons | = mass number − atomic number |
| Electron shells (elements 1–20) | fill 2, 8, 8 · e.g. Na (11) = 2,8,1 · Cl (17) = 2,8,7 |
| Group / period | Group = outer electrons (vertical) · Period = number of shells (horizontal) |

## Sheet 2 · Ions & Ionic Formulae (T1 Unit 1)

| Rule | Example |
|---|---|
| Groups 1–3 **lose** electrons (number lost = group number) → positive ions | Na⁺, Mg²⁺, Al³⁺ |
| Groups 6–7 **gain** electrons (number gained = 8 − group number) → negative ions | O²⁻, Cl⁻ |
| Total positive charge = total negative charge | Na⁺ + Cl⁻ → **NaCl** |
| | Mg²⁺ + 2Cl⁻ → **MgCl₂** |
| | 2Li⁺ + O²⁻ → **Li₂O** |
| | Ca²⁺ + 2Cl⁻ → **CaCl₂** · Ca²⁺ + O²⁻ → **CaO** |
| | 2Al³⁺ + 3O²⁻ → **Al₂O₃** |

Bonding types: ionic = electrons transferred · covalent = electrons shared (non-metals; e.g. F₂, H₂O, double bond in SO₂) · metallic = sea of free electrons.

## Sheet 3 · Photosynthesis (T1 Unit 2)

**Word equation:**

carbon dioxide + water → glucose + oxygen  *(light energy written above the arrow, absorbed by chlorophyll in chloroplasts)*

Reactants come in: CO₂ from the air, water from the soil via roots. Products: glucose (used for respiration, cellulose, starch, proteins, fats/oils) and oxygen (released).

Investigations: light intensity → count bubbles per minute from pondweed · starch test → iodine turns blue-black.

## Sheet 4 · Density (T2 Unit 3)

| Want | Formula | Units |
|---|---|---|
| Density | **ρ = m ÷ V** | g/cm³ (g and cm³) |
| Mass | **m = ρ × V** | g |
| Volume | **V = m ÷ ρ** | cm³ |
| Cuboid volume | V = length × width × height | cm³ |
| Irregular solid volume | displacement = final level − starting level | 1 mL = 1 cm³ |

Conversions: 1 dm³ = 1000 cm³ · 1 g/cm³ = 1000 kg/m³ (e.g. aluminium 2.7 g/cm³ = 2700 kg/m³).

Float or sink: less dense than the liquid → floats · denser → sinks.
Heating → particles spread out → density decreases · cooling → density increases.
Reference densities (g/cm³): water 1 · aluminium 2.7 · gold 19.32 · ice 0.9168 · liquid water 0.9971 · water vapour 0.000588 · hydrogen 0.000089.

## Sheet 5 · Waves, Sound & Thermal Energy (T2 Unit 3)

| Quantity | Formula / fact |
|---|---|
| Period and frequency | **T = 1 ÷ f** and **f = 1 ÷ T** (T in s, f in Hz) |
| Amplitude | height from the middle line to a crest/trough (bigger = **louder**) |
| Frequency | waves per second, Hz (higher = **higher pitch**) |
| Wavelength | distance between two consecutive crests / compressions |
| Constructive interference | crest meets crest → amplitudes **add** (2 cm + 3 cm = 5 cm) |
| Destructive interference | crest meets trough → amplitudes **cancel** |

Waves: transverse (vibrate at right angles; crest/trough) · longitudinal (parallel; compressions/rarefactions; sound).

Thermal energy: heat measured in joules (J) · temperature in °C (average kinetic energy of particles) · heat flows hot → cold.
Transfer: **conduction** (contact, solids/metals) · **convection** (fluids; warm rises, cool sinks; not in solids) · **radiation** (infrared, works in a vacuum).
Evaporation: surface only, any temperature, cools the liquid · Boiling: at boiling point, bubbles throughout.

## Sheet 6 · Electricity (T2 Unit 3)

| Quantity | Symbol | Unit | Meter |
|---|---|---|---|
| Voltage | V | volt (V) | voltmeter, in **parallel** |
| Current | I | ampere (A) | ammeter, in **series** |
| Resistance | R | ohm (Ω) | — |

**Ohm's law:** **V = I × R** · **I = V ÷ R** · **R = V ÷ I** (formula triangle: V on top, I and R along the bottom).

| | Series | Parallel |
|---|---|---|
| Current | same everywhere | divides between branches |
| Voltage | divides between components | same across each branch |
| Total resistance | **R_total = R₁ + R₂ + …** (e.g. 3 × 4 Ω = 12 Ω) | — |
| Reliability | one fails, all fail | more reliable (house wiring) |

Worked checks: 12 V, 3 A → R = 4 Ω · 240 V, 10 Ω → I = 24 A · 0.5 A, 16 Ω → V = 8 V · adding a second 10 Ω in series on 240 V → R = 20 Ω, I = 12 A.
Fixed resistor: R = V ÷ I stays roughly constant (small differences from measurement error) · variable resistor changes R, so changes current and lamp brightness.
